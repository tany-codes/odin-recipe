Recipe For Chole Bhature

Description of the dish:

Chole Bhature is a classic and popular Punjabi meal of a spicy, tangy chickpea curry (chole) paired with a soft, fluffy, deep-fried leavened bread (bhature). This hearty and indulgent combination of Chole Bhature is often enjoyed with pickles, onions, and lemon and is a popular choice for breakfast or brunch across North India.

Chole Bhature (or Chana Bhatura) is a popular North Indian dish made with spicy chickpea curry (chole) and deep-fried leavened bread (bhature).It's a hearty and satisfying meal, commonly eaten for breakfast or brunch in Delhi and Punjab, but enjoyed across India at any time of day, from street stalls to home kitchens.

Ingredients Needed:

  • 1½ cups all-purpose flour (maida)
  • ⅓ cup sooji (rava or fine semolina)
  • 2 tablespoons oil or ghee
  • 1 tablespoon granulated fine sugar
  • ½ teaspoon salt or as required
  • ¼ teaspoon baking soda
  • ½ teaspoon baking powder
  • ½ cup curd (yogurt)
  • 2 to 3 tablespoons water for kneading or add as needed
  • oil for deep frying

Instructions:

  1. Soak the chickpeas overnight or use boiled chickpeas.
  2. Prepare the onion, tomato, and spice mixture.
  3. Add the chickpeas and cook until the gravy thickens.
  4. Mix flour, yogurt, oil, and water to make a soft dough.
  5. Let the dough rest for about 2 hours.
  6. Divide the dough into small balls and roll them out.
  7. Deep-fry the bhature until they puff up and turn golden brown.
  8. Serve the hot chole with fresh bhature, onions, and pickle.
