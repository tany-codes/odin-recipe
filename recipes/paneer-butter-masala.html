Recipe For Paneer Butter Masala

Description:

Paneer Butter Masala is an iconic North Indian dish renowned for its luxurious, velvety texture and balanced flavor profile. The curry features succulent cubes of paneer—a fresh, non-melting Indian cottage cheese—submerged in a vibrant, orange-hued gravy. The base is crafted from a smooth purée of ripe tomatoes and aromatic aromatics, simmered extensively with a generous amount of butter. Cashew paste and fresh cream are integrated into the sauce, creating a thick, glossy consistency that coats the palate.

The flavor of this dish is characterized by a delicate harmony of mild sweetness, tanginess, and subtle heat. A blend of classic spices, including Kashmiri red chili powder, garam masala, and ginger-garlic paste, infuses the gravy with warmth without overpowering spiciness. The defining finishing touch comes from kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), which imparts a distinct earthy aroma and restaurant-style depth. This indulgent vegetarian staple is traditionally served hot alongside leavened flatbreads like butter naan or fragrant basmati rice.

Ingredients Needed:

  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • ½ pound paneer, cut into ½-inch cubes
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 onions, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon ginger paste
  • 1 teaspoon garlic paste
  • 1 tablespoon ground cashews
  • 1 teaspoon ground red chiles
  • ½ teaspoon ground cumin
  • ½ teaspoon ground coriander
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 (8 ounce) can tomato sauce
  • ½ cup half-and-half
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ teaspoon white sugar
  • ½ teaspoon salt

Steps:

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Heat oil in a large skillet over medium heat; fry paneer in batches until golden, about 5 minutes.
  3. Transfer fried paneer to a paper towel-lined plate to drain, retaining vegetable oil in skillet.
  4. Melt butter in the same skillet over medium heat; cook and stir onion until golden brown, about 10 minutes.
  5. Add ginger paste and garlic paste. Continue to cook until fragrant, about 1 minute more.
  6. Stir cashews, ground red chiles, cumin, coriander, and garam masala into the onion mixture. Cook and stir for 1 minute.
  7. Stir tomato sauce, half-and-half, milk, sugar, and salt into spice mixture; simmer until thickened, about 5 minutes.
  8. Reduce heat to low. Add fried paneer and simmer until heated through, about 5 minutes more.
